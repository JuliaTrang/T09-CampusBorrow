\section{Vision}

\subsection{Preliminary Problem Statement}
\textbf{Business context and problem.} CampusBorrow addresses equipment lending and classroom booking within a simulated university unit. Students and club representatives request shared equipment or rooms for academic and club activities, while designated staff manage approvals and equipment handovers. The project has not surveyed an actual unit; the workflows and problems below are preliminary business assumptions to be reviewed during requirements analysis, not findings from field research.

In the assumed workflow, requests arrive through separate forms or messages, while resource information is maintained in spreadsheets. Fragmented records make availability difficult to determine. Managers compare requests with existing records and update them manually, so delayed or inconsistent updates can result in duplicate equipment approvals or overlapping room bookings.

Equipment lending requires a distinction between approval and physical handover. Without clear records of reservation, handover, and return, managers may not know who holds an item or when it should be recovered. Overdue loans and approved requests that are never collected can tie up resources. Unstructured approval and status histories make decisions harder to trace and errors harder to correct.

\textbf{Business objectives and supported workflows.} CampusBorrow brings the resource catalogue, requests, and business history into one role-based web application for basic equipment lending and classroom booking. Borrowers find resources and track requests; managers process requests and monitor resource use within their assigned scope.

Each equipment request covers one item and specifies its purpose and return deadline. Approval checks availability and reserves the item consistently. A confirmed pickup appointment governs handover. Borrowers may cancel pending or approved requests until handover; after collection, the item must be returned. An approved request is automatically cancelled and its reservation released if handover has not occurred within 24 hours after the currently valid confirmed pickup appointment. Resource Managers record actual handover and return times; the system updates availability and identifies overdue loans. Borrowers contact the Resource Manager outside the application to request a different pickup time; only a Resource Manager may change the appointment. An in-application change-request workflow is optional and does not require chat.

For room bookings, users view approved time slots and request one room on one day with start and end times, purpose, and attendance. The system validates time and capacity and prevents approval of overlapping bookings. Borrowers may cancel before the start time. A room under maintenance is unavailable for new requests. If maintenance is recorded after approval, the unit handles the existing booking outside the system; automatic cancellation or reassignment is excluded.

\textbf{Users and management responsibilities.} The three application roles are Borrower, Resource Manager, and Admin. Borrowers manage their own requests. Resource Managers manage assigned resources and process requests and handovers. Admins manage accounts, roles, and assignments and may process requests, record handovers and returns, and correct erroneous states across the system. Pickup-time changes remain restricted to Resource Managers. History records business events and state changes, not every click or page view.

\textbf{Operating environment and constraints.} The product is a responsive web application accessed through a modern HTML5-capable browser on a computer or smartphone with a network connection. A server and central database support the course demonstration. The proposed stack is React with Vite, NestJS on Node.js, PostgreSQL hosted on Supabase, and Prisma. Deployment uses Vercel and Cloudflare; GitHub Actions supports continuous integration.

\textbf{Out of scope.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Academic timetable integration, event management, and event registration.
  \item Recurring room bookings and multiple items or rooms in one request.
  \item Advance equipment scheduling across future time slots and waiting lists. A pickup appointment coordinates handover of an already approved and reserved item.
  \item Repair workflows, maintenance work orders, and maintenance costs; only resource availability is recorded.
  \item Automatic cancellation, room reassignment, or rescheduling of approved bookings after a room is marked under maintenance; the unit handles these cases outside the application.
  \item Payments, deposits, and monetary penalties.
  \item Automated email/SMS notifications, single sign-on, real-time chat, multi-campus or multi-unit deployment, and native mobile applications.
\end{itemize}
